Your Business Hub — Easy Guide

Customers, quotes, and follow-ups in one place. No computer skills needed.

1. Open it

I put a button (an "icon") on your phone screen for you. Just tap it, the same way you open your text messages.

You can also open it on a computer. If you ever lose the button, the web address is:

https://superwrldcoin.github.io/ai-automation-service/clients/summit-comfort-hvac/

💡 You do not need to download or install anything, and it works with no cell signal — in a basement, in a crawlspace, out in the country.

2. What the buttons down the side do

Five things, and you'll only really use three of them:

  • 🏠 Home — your money at a glance, and who to call back today.
  • 👥 Customers — everyone you've worked for, and their history.
  • 📋 Quotes & Jobs — every price you've given, and where it stands.
  • 🔔 Follow-ups — the important one. Who owes you an answer.
  • 📅 Schedule — the jobs you've won, by date.
💡 On a phone, tap the ☰ lines in the top-left corner to show this menu.

3. Make a quote (about a minute)

Tap the ＋ New quote button at the top of the screen.

1

Pick the customer from the list — or choose "+ Add a new customer" and type their name.

2

Type the address and tap 🔎 Look up. It checks the address and adds your drive-time charge automatically.

3

Tick the boxes next to the work you're doing. Change any price right there if this job is different.

4

Tap Save & make PDF. Your finished quote appears.

4. The shortcut — let it fill the quote in for you

At the top of the New quote screen there's a yellow box. This is the fastest way to work.

Paste what the customer told you — their text message, their email, whatever they said — then tap ✨ Read it & fill the quote.

It reads it and fills in the quote for you: the work, how many, and their name, phone and address. You just check it and hit Save.

Tap 📎 Choose file instead if they sent you a job sheet or a PDF.

💡 It's reading words, not thinking — so always glance over what it filled in before you save. If it got something wrong, tap ↩ Undo and it puts everything back.

5. Send it to the customer

1

Tap Print / Save as PDF.

2

Choose Save as PDF. That makes a neat file with your name on it.

3

Text or email that file to your customer, just like sending a photo.

💡 After the job is done, open the same quote and tap 🧾 Invoice PDF. Same numbers — it just becomes a bill.

6. Never lose a job again

This is the part that pays for the tool. Every quote you save goes on your 🔔 Follow-ups list, and the red number beside it tells you how many people are waiting on you.

1

Open Follow-ups each morning.

2

Call anyone in a red or yellow row. Red means overdue.

3

Tap Won, Lost, or +3d (to be reminded again in three days).

💡 The number at the top is the dollars sitting in quotes nobody has answered. That's money most people forget about. Work this list and it pays for itself.

7. Win the job

Tap Won and set a date. It moves into your 📅 Schedule, and shows up on your Home screen under "Upcoming jobs".

8. Save a copy of your information

Please read this one. Your customers and quotes are saved on this device only — that's what keeps them private. But it also means that if you lose the phone, or something wipes the browser's storage, the information is gone.

So every couple of weeks: Settings → ⬇ Export backup. It saves one small file. Email it to yourself and you're covered. The Home screen will remind you.

To move to a new phone: put the app on the new phone, then Settings → ⬆ Import backup and pick that file. Everything comes back. I'll do this with you if you'd rather — just ask.

9. Your prices

Your service list and prices are already in there. To change them: Settings → Pricebook, edit any line, then Save pricebook. New quotes use the new prices.

Your information is private. Everything you type stays on your own phone or computer. It is not posted online, not on my computer, and not shared with anyone.
